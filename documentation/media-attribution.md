## Adding Media

Before adding another image or video:

1. Record its creator, source, and scientific provenance.
2. Confirm that the laboratory and data contributors approve public display.
3. State any crop, normalization, annotation, timing, or compositing change.
4. Keep unpublished strain identifiers and other nonpublic metadata out of filenames, captions, and embedded labels.
5. Add publisher figures only when their current license or written permission allows the intended use.

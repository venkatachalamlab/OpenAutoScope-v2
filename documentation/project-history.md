# Project History and Contributions

OpenAutoScope v2 was developed to follow an individual *C. elegans* over long periods while recording behavior and intermittent neural fluorescence. The project involved microscope design and assembly, electronics, control software, neural-network tracking, and subsequent data analysis.

## Initial System Development

Mahdi Torkashvand initiated and led the system's design and development. His work included the mechanical and optical design, primary assembly, control-software architecture and implementation, hardware integration, calibration, and creation of the initial training datasets. Mahdi manually annotated approximately 17,000 frames to establish the first terminal-bulb tracking model.

## Continued Development

Sina Rasouli contributed to assembly and software development during the shared development period. Mahdi and Sina collaborated on expanding and augmenting the training data. Sina led training and refinement of the tracking models and their integration into the instrument software.

After Mahdi left the laboratory in 2025, Sina led further modifications to the system, data collection, and analysis. The long-term dataset presented in this repository was collected and analyzed by Sina.
